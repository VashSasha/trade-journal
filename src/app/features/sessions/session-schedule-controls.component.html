About session hours

Asia / Overnight defaults to 5 PM–2 AM Central, an editable NVZN reference—not Tokyo exchange hours. Changes affect the header and bells, not trade dates or P&L.

Daylight saving is automatic. Windows with a boundary in a missing spring-forward hour are skipped. Holidays and early closes are not included. Preferences sync to your account.
